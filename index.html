Our Story

A Taste of Tradition,
A Touch of Modernity

Nestled in the heart of L MDINA, La Maison Gourmande has been welcoming guests since 2010. Our chefs blend the rich spices of Morocco, the elegance of Italian cuisine, and the comfort of fast food into a unique culinary experience.

Every dish is prepared with fresh, locally-sourced ingredients and served with warmth and hospitality — because for us, a meal is more than food, it's a memory.
